What your 24 weeks look like

    
The full-stack track, week by week. Other tracks follow the same rhythm with their own tools.

  

  

    
01Weeks 1-4
Foundations
Logic, one language, Git and the terminal. You write small programs every day.

    
02Weeks 5-12
Core skills
Frameworks, databases and APIs, taught with weekly code reviews.

    
03Weeks 13-20
Real projects
Teams of three ship a product with users, issues and deadlines.

    
04Weeks 21-24
Placement prep
Mock interviews, resume and portfolio work, plus data structures practice.
